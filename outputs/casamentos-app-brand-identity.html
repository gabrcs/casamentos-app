01

Strategic core

Product

A bespoke wedding-site service with RSVP, fee-free Pix gift lists, and an admin panel for couples.

Primary audience

Design-conscious engaged couples in Curitiba and the surrounding region, initially, who are 4–14 months from their wedding and are choosing suppliers who can make the occasion feel personal.

Problem

Couples must choose between a generic-looking wedding site and an experience that reduces the money received from their guests; neither feels worthy of a carefully planned celebration.

Category

Bespoke wedding-site service.

Current alternatives

Template platforms, a manually assembled link page, an invitation without RSVP management, or doing without a wedding site.

Positioning

For design-conscious engaged couples preparing a wedding, casamentos.app is the bespoke wedding-site service that gives their celebration a personal online home and preserves 100% of Pix gift value, because each site is built around the couple and includes RSVP, direct Pix gifts, and simple self-management—unlike generic template platforms or a patchwork of links.

Salient brand idea
A wedding site that belongs to the couple.
Brand promise

Your celebration online, unmistakably yours—and your gifts remain entirely yours.

Proof points
  • The reusable product includes a working RSVP database, Pix BR Code generation, an administrative panel, and gift-list management.
  • A real Marcus & Daíse wedding site is authorised for use as the first public case.
  • A first paid project at R$500 validates an entry price, not yet the final pricing ceiling.
Personality
  • Warm, not sugary
  • Elegant, not precious
  • Direct, not transactional
  • Personal, not performative

Landing-page hero

Hero shows one real couple site inside the asymmetric frame. The supporting line names RSVP and direct Pix; the primary CTA opens WhatsApp. The page explains the service before the tagline.

Favicon and social avatar

The frame, reduced.

Use only the simple asymmetrical frame in Plum on Ivory or Ivory on Plum. Test at 16, 24, and 32 pixels; do not attempt to render the full wordmark at favicon size.

Do
  • Lead with a real couple, a real site screen, or a concrete product moment.
  • Use casamentos.app consistently in profile name, bio, landing page, footer attribution, and public sales material.
  • Repeat the direct-Pix benefit with exact, plain language.
  • Show visibly different couple sites to prove bespoke work.
  • Keep all body text in Cocoa on Ivory or Ivory on Plum for reliable readability.
Don't
  • Do not use BG Casamentos in public-facing identity once migration begins.
  • Do not fill the feed with generic wedding photography without a service or product point.
  • Do not describe the service as a platform or rely on unsupported claims about competitors.
  • Do not use Antique Gold or Rose Clay for small body text on Ivory.
  • Do not treat the proposed frame symbol as trademark-cleared or a finished production logo.
06

Validation plan

Strengths
  • The service has functional product proof, not only a concept.
  • The no-fee direct Pix mechanism is concrete and easy to explain.
  • The first case can make bespoke work visible immediately.
Assumptions
  • The first acquisition focus remains Instagram discovery and WhatsApp conversion.
  • Curitiba is the initial geographic focus, with national service possible later.
  • The first public case remains authorised for portfolio use.
Risks
  • One public case may not yet prove range; make contrast across future sites visible as soon as possible.
  • The direct-Pix message must be worded accurately and reviewed against the actual implementation and customer terms.
  • casamentos.app may require a preliminary name and trademark search before substantial launch investment.
Next tests
  • Five-second recognition: show the pinned case-post cover to engaged couples and ask what the service does.
  • Audience comprehension: ask whether viewers understand both bespoke design and direct Pix after one carousel.
  • One-week recall: ask whether viewers remember ‘Entirely yours’, the frame, or fee-free Pix.
  • Monochrome test: print the frame mark in one colour and confirm it remains recognisable.
  • Favicon test: compare the mark at 16–32px against other wedding-service icons.
  • Landing-message test: test the hero against the current headline for WhatsApp click-through quality.
  • Competitor-confusion check: shuffle anonymised posts with wedding suppliers and confirm casamentos.app is correctly identified.
